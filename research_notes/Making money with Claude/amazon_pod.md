## 1. Merch on Demand product catalog and marketplaces

### Takeaway
Merch on Demand (formerly Merch by Amazon) covers apparel (t-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, tank tops, and more recently polos and quarter-zips), plus phone cases, PopSockets, throw pillows, tote bags, tumblers and water bottles. It sells into 7 Amazon marketplaces (US, UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, JP). **No source says Merch on Demand offers rugs or doormats.** Rugs sold "on Amazon" with custom designs almost certainly come from third-party POD suppliers (Printify, Printful, Teelaunch, PrintKK, etc.) linked to a Seller Central account, not from Merch on Demand.

### Cited Findings
- Sellers upload artwork and apply it to t-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, tank tops, phone cases and PopSockets — [AMZPrep 2026 guide](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/); [Nexscope 2026](https://www.nexscope.ai/blog/amazon-merch-on-demand)
- Merch on Demand also offers throw pillows (4500x4500px template; reported royalties $6.18–9.63) and tote bags (4500x5400px; reported royalties $4.30–7.82). These royalty ranges come from a Merch Informer article that is **likely older (pre-2026)** — [Merch Informer, phone cases & PopSockets](https://merchinformer.com/the-case-for-cell-phone-cases-and-popsockets/); [Merch Informer listings](https://merchinformer.com/merch-amazon-listings/)
- In June 2026 Amazon launched a consumer-facing AI design feature (Alexa in the Amazon Shopping app) that prints on Merch on Demand products: "T-shirts, hoodies, polo shirts, quarter zips, tumblers, and water bottles." It is free to use, customers pay only for the product, and it is US-only — [TechCrunch, 2026-06-08](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/08/amazon-now-lets-you-design-custom-merch-using-ai/); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/amazon-wants-to-end-dodgy-knockoffs-with-its-own-ai-generated-custom-merch-printing?rand=100); [Westfield/goodfest mirror](https://goodfest.westfield.com/2026/06/amazon-now-lets-you-design-custom-merch-using-ai/)
- Marketplaces: Amazon.com, .co.uk, .de, .fr, .it, .es, .co.jp — [AMZPrep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/)
- Reported production costs run 8–12% higher in EU marketplaces than the US, and 15–20% higher in Japan — [AMZPrep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/) (third-party claim)
- A Merch Titans "product types guide 2026" exists, but I could not read its contents — [Merch Titans products guide](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-on-demand-product-types-guide)
- Merch Informer's "Scaling beyond T-shirts" covers Merch's non-apparel products — [Merch Informer](https://merchinformer.com/?p=11044)

### Inferences
- Tumblers and water bottles are confirmed Merch products, because the June 2026 AI feature prints on them. One search summary lists "mugs," but no primary source confirms that ceramic mugs are a Merch product. Mugs on Amazon from independent designers are probably sold mostly through Seller Central + POD (Printful/Printify/Gelato/etc.).
- Rugs and doormats are **not** in any Merch catalog I found. What the user saw on Amazon is almost certainly Seller Central + POD, with suppliers such as Printify, Printful, Teelaunch or PrintKK making the product (see section 5).

### Gaps
- I could not access the official, current Merch product list (merch.amazon.com resources are blocked). It is unconfirmed whether Merch offers ceramic mugs, stickers or other home goods in 2026, and which products are available in each marketplace.

## 2. Application, waitlist, tiers and upload limits

### Takeaway
You still have to apply and be approved. The tier ladder (10 → 25 → 100 → 500 → 1,000 → 2,000+ design slots) is still in place, and sales move you up. Third-party blogs cite approval odds and timelines, but I found no Amazon source to confirm them.

### Cited Findings
- Tier ladder: Tier 10 (10 designs), 25, 100, 500, 1000, 2000+. Each tier comes with upload and daily publishing limits — [BeBold Digital, tiers](https://www.bebolddigital.com/blog/amazon-merch-tiers); [Merch Informer, tiers](https://merchinformer.com/?p=8494)
- To tier up you need both sales and filled slots. Reported rough thresholds: ~10 sales for Tier 25, ~25 for Tier 100, ~100 for Tier 500, ~500 for Tier 1000 — [BeBold Digital](https://www.bebolddigital.com/blog/amazon-merch-tiers) (approximate; Amazon does not publish exact criteria)
- Amazon reviews applications manually in 2–8 weeks, and approval rates in 2026 are "around 30 to 40%" — [AMZPrep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/) (**unverified**; Amazon does not publish approval rates, so treat this as a blog estimate)
- Listings with no sales in 18 months are now deleted automatically (reported as a 2026 change) — [AMZPrep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/)
- Amazon reportedly changed Merch listing requirements in Q1 2026. One blog claims "Motion Rank" (video/animated mockups) is now an A9 ranking input — [search snippet attributed to Merch Titans review](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-on-demand-review) (**dubious**: I found no Amazon confirmation, and the claim may be marketing for mockup tools)
- Guide to getting approved — [Merch Titans account guide](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-account-guide) (content not read)

### Inferences
- A new account starts at 10 design slots. Expect months of slow scaling before volume uploading is possible, which limits the "mass-produce AI designs" approach early on.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative 2026 statement on whether there is a formal waitlist or invite-only status. I also found no tier-specific daily upload caps (historically roughly 1–8/day at low tiers, but I could not verify that for 2026).

## 3. Royalty structure and payment

### Takeaway
**This is the biggest change of 2026.** From June 1, 2026, Merch replaced its flat royalty with three "royalty incentive groups" based on the share of sales driven by non-organic (external or ads) traffic. Sellers who rely only on Amazon's organic search reportedly had their royalties roughly **halved**: about $2.44 on a $19.99 tee at the default tier.

### Cited Findings
- Effective June 1, 2026: three tiers based on a trailing two-month (60-day) average of non-organic, traffic-driven sales — [MyDesigns, royalty changes 2026](https://mydesigns.io/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-2026/); [Merch Titans, June 2026 changes](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-june-2026)
  - **Creator Tier** (default, organic only): 50% of the standard royalty rate
  - **Plus Tier**: ≥15% of monthly unit sales from non-organic sources, tracked via Amazon Attribution links; pays 2x the Creator/base royalty
  - **Premium Tier**: ≥35% non-organic; pays 108% of the Plus rate (≈2.16x Creator)
  - "Non-organic" includes social media, email, content traffic and Amazon Sponsored Products ads — [Merch Titans](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-june-2026)
- Example: a $19.99 tee earns $2.44 at Creator Tier and $4.88 at Plus Tier — [Pixels N' Profits, "Is Amazon Merch worth it 2026"](https://pixelsnprofits.com/amazon-merch-worth-it-2026/); [Nexscope](https://www.nexscope.ai/blog/amazon-merch-on-demand)
- Older/flat-rate reference figures for a US standard tee (likely **pre-June 2026**): $2.21 at $15.99, $3.77 at $19.99, $5.33 at $23.99. Royalties across products range from $0.37 to $13.07 per sale — [AMZPrep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/). **Conflict:** $4.88 at Plus Tier is higher than the old $3.77 flat figure. This fits the "Plus pays more than before" framing, but no source I read confirms the exact baseline.
- "If your sales come mostly from Amazon's own traffic, your rate roughly got cut in half" — [Pixels N' Profits](https://pixelsnprofits.com/amazon-merch-worth-it-2026/)
- Royalty calculator (third-party) — [Merch Titans calculator](https://merchtitans.com/tools/amazon-merch-royalty-calculator)
- Per-product royalty guide — [Merch Titans royalties guide](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-on-demand-royalties-guide) (not read)

### Inferences
- Under the new model, pure "upload AI designs and let Amazon search sell them" earns about $2.44 per standard tee. To earn more you need an external audience (TikTok, Pinterest, email) or paid Sponsored Products ads, which cut into margins.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm payment terms for 2026. Historically payment was monthly, about 29 days after the end of the month, by direct deposit, but I could not verify this against the 2026 Amazon FAQ.
- I found no royalty figures for tumblers or water bottles, and no official Amazon announcement text for the June 2026 change (only third-party summaries).

## 4. AI-generated designs: policy, IP enforcement, terminations

### Takeaway
AI-generated designs are **not banned** on Merch on Demand. Amazon itself now makes AI-generated merch for shoppers. The seller carries full responsibility for rights, IP and content policy. Some third-party sites say to disclose AI use, but I found **no confirmed Merch-specific AI disclosure field** (unlike KDP). Trademark or IP violations can lead to permanent termination, and enforcement is reported to be stricter in 2025–26.

### Cited Findings
- AI designs are permitted under standard Amazon content policies. In Amazon's own AI merch tool, automated moderation flags possible IP infringement, and designs that imply ties to branded properties (sports teams, franchises) are often rejected — [TechCrunch 2026-06-08](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/08/amazon-now-lets-you-design-custom-merch-using-ai/); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/amazon-wants-to-end-dodgy-knockoffs-with-its-own-ai-generated-custom-merch-printing?rand=100)
- "Amazon's policy does not mention a specific 'AI' disclosure field, but puts responsibility on the creator to hold the necessary rights" — this applies to the design and to the title, description, bullets, keywords and brand name — [search summary of MerchBanao / RemoveAILabel](https://merchbanao.com/blog/amazon-merch-ai-policy/). **Contradiction:** [RemoveAILabel](https://removeailabel.com/blog/merch-by-amazon-ai-generated-designs-policy) says Merch "requires accurate AI disclosure in listing fields." That site sells AI-metadata-stripping tools, so its claims are self-interested. I treat the disclosure requirement as **unconfirmed**.
- RemoveAILabel claims that files with strong AI metadata (e.g., C2PA) can trigger review or rejection — [RemoveAILabel](https://removeailabel.com/blog/merch-by-amazon-ai-generated-designs-policy) (unverified, self-interested source)
- Official Merch content policy page — [merch.amazon.com/resource/201858630](https://merch.amazon.com/resource/201858630) (fetch blocked; contents not verified)
- Compliance checklist (trademarks, copyright, content policy) — [SellerSprite](https://sellersprite.ai/en/blog/amazon-merch-design-compliance-checklist)
- Merch terminations are described as permanent: you lose all listings, reviews and sales, and one trademark or content violation can end the account without appeal. Designs are "getting flagged for trademark issues that would've passed two years ago" — [Merch Titans trademark guide](https://merchtitans.com/blog/how-to-avoid-trademark-infringement-amazon-merch-on-demand); [Merch Titans account guide](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-account-guide)
- Legal commentary on Merch terminations and DMCA disputes — [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/navigating-amazon-merch-on-demand-account-termination-and-dmca-disputes); [Vondran Legal via JDSupra](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/was-your-amazon-merch-on-demand-account-5924644/); [Amazon forum reinstatement thread](https://www.amazonforum.com/s/question/0D56Q0000BrB8lBSQS/request-for-amazon-merch-on-demand-account-reinstatement)

### Inferences
- AI image generators often reproduce trademarked phrases, characters or styles. Because enforcement is strict and there is no appeal, the main risk for AI-assisted sellers is IP, not the AI origin itself. Running a trademark check (e.g., USPTO TESS) on text before uploading is standard practice.
- Amazon's June 2026 consumer AI-merch tool now competes directly with sellers: shoppers can generate their own "funny cat shirt" instead of buying a seller's design. Sellers of generic, low-effort AI designs are the most exposed.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the exact wording of any Merch AI disclosure question or checkbox in the 2026 upload flow.

## 5. Alternative route: Seller Central + third-party POD (where the rugs and mugs come from)

### Takeaway
With a Seller Central Professional account ($39.99/mo) plus a POD integration (Printify, Printful, Gelato, and via API or other routes CustomCat, SPOD, Teelaunch), you can list almost any POD product on Amazon, **including rugs, doormats, mugs and tumblers**. The POD supplier prints and ships (merchant-fulfilled). You pay Amazon's referral fee (about 15% for home/kitchen) and set your own price and margin. This is almost certainly how the rugs the user saw are sold. Amazon Handmade prohibits POD.

### Cited Findings
- The Professional plan costs $39.99/month and is required for the Printify integration (API access, automated order processing) — [Printify help: costs of selling on Amazon](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/43556915343633-What-are-the-costs-associated-with-selling-on-Amazon); [Printify Amazon seller fees](https://printify.com/blog/amazon-seller-fees/)
- The referral fee for Home & Kitchen items such as mugs is 15% — [Printify seller fees](https://printify.com/blog/amazon-seller-fees/); [Printful help: Amazon referral fees](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014067159-What-are-Amazon-s-referral-fees)
- Example cost from a margin write-up: Printful mug $8.95 + $3.99+ shipping; Amazon takes 15% plus $39.99/mo — [Bootstrapping Ecommerce](https://bootstrappingecommerce.com/selling-on-amazon-with-printful)
- Printify launched a direct Amazon integration for US sellers in 2024 and has 2,000+ products — [Printify: how to sell POD on Amazon 2026](https://printify.com/blog/how-to-sell-with-print-on-demand-on-amazon/); [Printify Amazon POD](https://printify.com/amazon-print-on-demand/)
- Printful has an Amazon integration covering t-shirts, mugs, accessories and more — [Printful Amazon integration](https://www.printful.com/integrations/amazon); [Printful guide](https://www.printful.com/blog/how-to-sell-on-amazon-with-printfuls-integration)
- Gelato launched an Amazon integration (US marketplace) covering wall art, calendars, tote bags and sportswear, with Gelato handling production and shipping — [The Dead Pixels Society](https://thedeadpixelssociety.com/gelato-launches-amazon-integration/); [Print Monthly](https://www.printmonthly.co.uk/?p=9570); [Gelato Amazon page](https://www.gelato.com/de/print-on-demand/amazon)
- "In 2026, Printful, Printify, and Gelato offer the most seamless direct integrations with Amazon Seller Central," and all require the Professional plan — [PrintOnDemandBusiness 2026](https://www.printondemandbusiness.com/blog/which-print-on-demand-companies-integrate-with-amazon-in-2025/)
- Suppliers for **rugs and doormats**: Printify (custom rugs, doormats, outdoor rugs), Printful (custom rugs), Teelaunch (coir outdoor doormats and polyester indoor mats), PrintKK (rugs and mats, no minimum), plus Gooten, CustomCat and others — [Printify custom rugs](https://printify.com/custom-rugs/); [Printify door mats](https://printify.com/custom-door-mats/); [Printify rugs & mats catalog](https://printify.com/app/products/home-and-living/rugs-and-mats); [Printful custom rugs](https://www.printful.com/custom-rugs); [PrintKK rugs](https://www.printkk.com/custom-rugs); [The Clever Business, doormat & rug POD list](https://thecleverbusiness.com/doormat-and-rug-print-on-demand/); [Passive Marketeer, POD rugs](https://passivemarketeer.com/print-on-demand-rugs/)
- Blogs cite 30/40 oz tumblers as the top viral POD category of 2026 (base cost ~$19.95, retail $38–48) and 11/15 oz mugs at $18–24 retail — [search summary; Merch Titans custom products guide](https://merchtitans.com/blog/custom-print-on-demand-products-guide) (blog estimates, before Amazon fees)
- **Amazon Handmade prohibits** outsourced production, drop-shipping and reselling, so POD is not allowed there. Amazon Custom (personalization) requires seller fulfillment, not FBA — [Amazon Handmade program page](https://sell.amazon.com/programs/handmade); [AdNabu Handmade guide](https://blog.adnabu.com/amazon/how-to-sell-on-amazon-handmade/)
- Comparison of the two paths (Merch vs Seller Central POD) — [MyDesigns: Amazon POD two paths 2026](https://mydesigns.io/blog/amazon-print-on-demand/); [PrintOnDemandBusiness: Merch, Seller Central or KDP](https://www.printondemandbusiness.com/blog/how-to-start-amazon-print-on-demand-in-2025/)

### Inferences
- Rough mug math (illustrative only, using the cited figures): sell at $19.99, pay a 15% referral fee (~$3.00), then Printful base plus shipping (~$12.94), leaving about $4 gross before the $39.99/mo plan fee, ads and returns. Rugs have higher base costs and larger shipping, so they need higher prices. Margins are thin unless listings rank or are advertised.
- Seller Central accounts face full Amazon seller obligations: identity verification, account health metrics, and late-shipment and valid-tracking-rate standards. POD production times can put these at risk.

### Gaps

- I have no confirmed SPOD Amazon integration status for 2026.

## 6. Saturation, competition and realistic earnings

### Takeaway
The consensus of 2026 sources is that Merch "still works but is no longer upload-and-forget." The June 2026 royalty cut, stricter IP enforcement, the 18-month listing purge, and Amazon's own AI design tool all reduce returns from high-volume generic (often AI) designs. Earnings figures come from blogs, not surveys, and are anecdotal.

### Cited Findings
- "Merch as a standalone passive income source is harder than it was"; serious sellers "consistently earn $500–$5,000+ per month" (blog claim, no data behind it) — [Pixels N' Profits](https://pixelsnprofits.com/amazon-merch-worth-it-2026/); [Wrench and Wallet 2026](https://wrenchandwallet.com/how-to-make-money-amazon-merch/)
- The winning 2026 strategy is multi-platform (Etsy, Redbubble, own store) plus external traffic to qualify for higher royalty tiers — [Pixels N' Profits](https://pixelsnprofits.com/amazon-merch-worth-it-2026/); [SmartPrintee, compliance & brand stores](https://smartprintee.com/en/blog/amazon-merch-2026-compliance-brand-stores)
- Suggested path: treat Merch as a catalog-testing phase, then move winners into a registered Amazon Brand Store — [SmartPrintee](https://smartprintee.com/en/blog/amazon-merch-2026-compliance-brand-stores)
- One Merch business was sold via Empire Flippers ("$93K in 19 days" headline; an outlier with unknown date) — [Empire Flippers](https://empireflippers.com/sell-merch-by-amazon-business/)
- Other 2026 reviews — [Merch Titans review](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-on-demand-review); [Stores Automation](https://storesautomation.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/); [The Selling Guys](https://www.thesellingguys.com/merch-by-amazon/); [Printify, "Is Merch worth it"](https://printify.com/knowledge-hub/is-amazon-merch-on-demand-worth-it/)

### Inferences
- For an AI-assisted newcomer in late 2026, expected returns from Merch alone are low. The first months are capped at 10–25 slots, a tee earns about $2.44 organically, and competition from mass AI uploads and Amazon's own generator is heavy. The economics favor niche designs, trademark-safe text, and an external audience.

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous survey data on median Merch seller earnings in 2025–2026. The earnings numbers are blog assertions.
- I could not access Reddit (r/MerchByAmazon, r/printondemand) or paid tools (Merch Informer, ProductorBot, Merch Dominator) for community sentiment or data.

### Takeaway
Key changes: the three-tier royalty model (June 1, 2026), auto-deletion of listings with no sales in 18 months, reported Q1 2026 listing-requirement changes, the June 2026 Alexa AI custom-merch feature on Merch products, and stricter trademark flagging.

### Cited Findings
- Royalty incentive groups effective June 1, 2026 — [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-2026/); [Merch Titans](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-june-2026)

- Amazon Shopping app / Alexa AI design tool for Merch products (US, June 2026) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/08/amazon-now-lets-you-design-custom-merch-using-ai/); [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/amazon-embeds-ai-merch-design-tool-in-shopping-app)
- Brand in naming: the program is now called "Merch on Demand" (formerly "Merch by Amazon") — [AMZPrep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/)

### Inferences
- Taken together, Amazon is pushing Merch sellers to act as marketers who bring outside traffic, and is itself taking over the "generic AI design" end of the market.
