## 1. Etsy: AI and POD policies, 2025-2026 changes, fees, saturation

### Takeaway
POD is still allowed on Etsy under the "Designed by a seller" category, as long as the seller's POD partner is disclosed as a production partner. AI-generated designs are allowed but must be disclosed, and Etsy's Creativity Standards push back on designs with little human input. Enforcement reportedly tightened in 2026. Fees usually take about 12-20% of each sale (take rate 25.9% including ads and services). The marketplace is growing again under a new CEO (GMS +7.5% YoY in Q2 2026), but it is crowded, with about 5.7M active sellers.

### Cited Findings
**Creativity Standards / production partners**
- Etsy created its Creativity Standards page in July 2024. It defines four seller roles: "Made by" (crafted, altered, or assembled by the seller), "Designed by" (original designs, including seller-prompted AI art, produced or printed by a third party), "Sourced by" (supplies and party items), and "Handpicked by" (vintage). — [Sarah Sewell Substack summary](https://sarahsewell.substack.com/p/etsys-creativity-standards-update); primary page: [Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity) (could not fetch directly); [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1276491338090)
- Printify maintains a help article for POD sellers on complying with Etsy's Creativity Standards. — [Printify Help](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4578896595345-What-should-I-know-about-Etsy-s-Creative-Standards-policy)
- Production partner disclosure is required for any seller using a third-party manufacturer, at both the account level and each listing. POD sellers must add their POD provider (Printify, Printful, and so on) as a production partner. — [ShieldMyShop 2026 guide](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-05-21-etsy-production-partner-disclosure-pod-guide); [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/understanding-etsys-rules-for-print-on-demand-sellers)
- "Using a print-on-demand service is still totally fine … the policy change isn't about who prints your products but about who creates the designs." Uploading stock graphics or AI images without meaningful creative input and proper disclosure violates the Seller Policy. — [LzyPOD](https://lzypod.com/blog/etsy-ai-policy-what-sellers-need-to-know/); [iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-creativity-standards-pod-sellers-guide)

**AI disclosure**
- Sellers must say in the listing description when an item was created with AI, and must set the attribution to "Designed by". — [inkfluenceai](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-explained-2026); [Artomate](https://www.artomate.app/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-policy-2026)
- Multiple 2026 blogs say an updated Seller Policy dated January 14, 2026 requires ticking an "I used AI-generative technology" checkbox, choosing "Designed by" instead of "Made by", and stating AI use in the description. **[secondary/unverified: the date and checkbox wording could not be confirmed against etsy.com]** — [Promptless Press](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-designed-by-ai-field); [ecombalance](https://ecombalance.com/ai-content-policies-2026/); [iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-creativity-standards-pod-sellers-guide)
- The Creativity Standards were rewritten so that anything made with computerized tools must be the seller's own original design. The crackdown reportedly bans licensed or purchased 3D models, Canva templates, and minimally modified designs. **[secondary/unverified]** — [iscompliant.app "Is Etsy still handmade"](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/is-etsy-still-handmade); [Listybox](https://listybox.com/blog/etsy-print-on-demand-rules-policies-disclosure-2026/)

**Crackdowns**
- Claim: Etsy removed about 12,000 listings and issued about 8,500 warnings in Q1 2026 for incorrect or missing AI disclosure, mostly affecting POD shops with undisclosed all-AI catalogs. **[secondary/unverified: I found no Etsy primary source for these figures; they appear on compliance-tool vendor blogs]** — [iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-creativity-standards-pod-sellers-guide); [ecombalance](https://ecombalance.com/ai-content-policies-2026/)
- CONFLICT: one search-surfaced source said increased POD shop suspensions happened "under new CEO Rachel Park". That is false. Treat the "Rachel Park" source as unreliable. — [Etsy IR press release](https://investors.etsy.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/212/etsy-announces-leadership-transition-kruti-patel-goyal-to-become-chief-executive-officer-josh-silverman-transitions-to-executive-chair-effective-january-1-2026)
- Sellers' mixed experience of Etsy's "human-centered" positioning is covered by EcomCrew. — [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/etsy-calls-itself-human-centered-its-sellers-have-a-more-complicated-story-to-tell/)

**Fees (2026)**
- $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee, and payment processing of about 3% + $0.25 in the US. Offsite Ads charge 15% on attributed sales for shops under $10k/year, which can opt out; the fee is 12% and mandatory for shops over $10k. In total Etsy takes about 12-20% per sale. — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [Gelato blog](https://www.gelato.com/blog/the-real-cost-of-selling-on-etsy); [sellerfeecalc](https://sellerfeecalc.com/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026)

**Marketplace scale and saturation (Q2 2026)**
- Etsy marketplace GMS was $2.6B in Q2 2026, up 7.5% YoY, the third straight quarter of growth. Revenue was $668M at a 25.9% take rate. Active buyers were about 87M, roughly flat YoY. Active sellers were about 5.706M, up 5.9% YoY. — [Etsy Q2 2026 shareholder letter (SEC 8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm); [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/article/etsy-revenue-gms/); [Yahoo Finance call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/etsy-inc-etsy-q2-2026-231603652.html)
- Retail TouchPoints reports that Goyal describes the marketplace rebuild as "paying off". — [Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/features/etsys-marketplace-rebuild-paying-off-says-ceo-kruti-patel-goyal/619194/)

### Inferences
- Seller count is growing (+5.9%) while buyers are flat (~87M), so each seller gets a smaller share of buyers. Competition per buyer is getting tighter, and AI makes it cheaper to list a lot, which likely makes this worse.
- A compliant AI + POD listing on Etsy in 2026 needs three things: "Designed by" attribution, production-partner disclosure, and an AI disclosure. Bulk-generated, low-effort catalogs are the most exposed to enforcement.
- The 25.9% take rate is higher than the 12-20% headline fee because it includes Etsy Ads, Offsite Ads, shipping labels, and similar services. Sellers who run ads should plan for more than 20%.

## 2. Redbubble, TeePublic, Society6, Zazzle, Spring: 2026 status and royalty models

### Takeaway
The traditional POD marketplaces have become much worse for small artists. Redbubble charges a large fee on lower tiers, Society6 is now invite-only with 5-10% royalties, Zazzle restructured its payouts, and Spring (Teespring) shut down on September 15, 2026. TeePublic, which is also owned by Articore, still pays flat royalties.

### Cited Findings
**Redbubble (Articore Group, ASX: ATG)**
- Redbubble is part of Articore Group Limited, listed on the ASX as ATG. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redbubble)
- Since September 1, 2025, fees are set by artist tier. Standard tier pays a platform fee of up to 50% of monthly earnings, Premium 20%, and Pro 0%. Pro is reserved for top performers and Artist Ambassadors. Fees are capped at $150/month. — [Redbubble blog, Aug 2025](https://blog.redbubble.com/2025/08/artist-account-tiers-and-fees/) (snippet only; fetch blocked); [Redbubble Help](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/50959863016724-How-does-my-Account-Tier-determine-my-account-fees); [simplelifecalc](https://simplelifecalc.com/guides/is-redbubble-still-profitable)
- Account fees were first introduced in 2023. — [Redbubble blog, Apr 2023](https://blog.redbubble.com/2023/04/how-account-fees-may-apply-to-you/)
- The 2025 fee changes make Redbubble "significantly less profitable for lower-volume artists." — [lavaritte review](https://lavaritte.com/blogs/redbubble-for-artists-review/); [homecynhome](https://www.homecynhome.com/post/redbubble-announced-a-change-in-fees-structures)

**TeePublic (also Articore)**
- Flat royalties with no platform fees. Artisan tier earns $4 per tee at full price and $2 during sales. Apprentice tier earns $3 at full price and $1 during sales. — [AutoKeyWorder](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/teepublic-vs-redbubble/)

**Society6**
- In early 2025 Society6 moved to a curated, invite-only model. Thousands of artist accounts were removed, with shops shut down by March 2025, and a second round of removals followed in October 2025. Artists earn a fixed 5-10% of the net sale depending on product and can no longer set prices. New artists must apply and can be dropped based on sales or "brand direction." — [ecommerceceo](https://www.ecommerceceo.com/best-society6-alternatives/); [wildlifeartstore](https://www.wildlifeartstore.com/sell-art-on-society6/); [printkk](https://www.printkk.com/blog/articles/redbubble-vs-society6)

**Zazzle**
- The Ambassador program was introduced in April 2025 with a new way of calculating royalties and referral earnings. Cross-promotion referral links pay 15% of net referral sales, and self-promotion links pay 35-50% depending on the product department. — [Zazzle Help (NZ)](https://www.zazzle.co.nz/hc/article/ambassador_referral_commissions_creator_royalties-30810357489175); [Zazzle royalty percentages](https://www.zazzle.ca/hc/article/zazzle_royalty_and_referral_percentages-360037785973)

**Spring (formerly Teespring)**
- Spring shut down in stages. On September 15, 2026, stores stopped taking orders, selling and product tools were removed, and the dashboard was limited to payouts. Amaze Commerce, the owner, is steering creators to its own platform. Search results also show Spring, Inc. listed as dissolved on September 15, 2026. — [Amaze Commerce "Spring Is Shutting Down"](https://www.amazecommerce.com/spring-update); [Wikipedia: Teespring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teespring)
- Spring had laid off staff and closed its Kentucky facility earlier (2023). — [Amanda Perelli LinkedIn/Business Insider](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/amandaperelli_creator-merch-company-spring-has-laid-off-activity-7019735511065903104-PdRH)

### Inferences
- A new seller in 2026 is likely to be pushed toward the "own your channel" model (Etsy, Shopify, or TikTok Shop plus Printify, Printful, or Gelato), where they set prices and margins, and away from royalty marketplaces with shrinking payouts.
- On Redbubble's Standard tier the fee can be up to 50%, which makes it roughly break-even for casual AI-design uploaders.

### Gaps
- No verified 2025-2026 traffic data (e.g., Similarweb) for Redbubble or Society6. The "declining traffic" claim could not be sourced.

## 3. Printify, Printful, Gelato: product range, base costs, margins; TikTok Shop and Shopify

### Takeaway
Printify is the cheapest option through its network of independent print providers. Printful costs more and fulfills in-house for more consistent quality. Gelato focuses on local production across many countries. Home goods such as doormats, rugs, and blankets are cheap to add, and mugs have the widest price gap between providers. Margin targets of 25-40% after all fees are typical.

### Cited Findings
- A Bella+Canvas 3001 tee costs about $9.04 on Printify Premium versus $11.69 at Printful, about 22.7% cheaper. A mug costs about $3.68 on Printify versus $7.56 at Printful. — [podprofitcheck.org](https://podprofitcheck.org/)
- Gelato's base cost for a US unisex tee is about $9-11. Gelato produces locally in 32 countries and routes about 90% of orders to a facility near the customer. Printify routes through 80+ independent providers, so quality varies. — [podprofitcheck.org](https://podprofitcheck.org/); [Syncost Gelato review](https://www.syncost.com/blogs/gelato-print-on-demand-review); [Printful: Gelato vs Printify](https://www.printful.com/blog/gelato-vs-printify)
- Recommended target margin is 25-40% after all costs including marketplace fees. Mugs, posters, and stickers have the strongest margins. — [Printful blog](https://www.printful.com/blog/what-is-a-good-profit-margin-for-print-on-demand); [Printify most profitable products](https://printify.com/blog/most-profitable-print-on-demand-products/)
- Printify home goods (EUR prices shown on the Printify Premium catalog): polyester doormat from EUR 8.06 (another doormat from EUR 17.77), area rugs from EUR 15.16, outdoor rugs from EUR 15.42, blankets from $12.53 or $15.24 depending on model. — [Printify doormat](https://printify.com/app/products/1601/generic-brand/polyester-doormat/); [Printify rugs and mats](https://printify.com/app/products/home-and-living/rugs-and-mats); [Printify blankets](https://printify.com/custom-blankets); [Printify doormat business guide](https://printify.com/knowledge-hub/how-to-start-print-on-demand-doormat-business/)
- Printify, Printful, and Gelato all integrate with Etsy, Shopify, and TikTok Shop. Gelato's TikTok Shop integration covers US and UK sellers. Printify added TikTok Shop (US) in 2024. — [Gelato TikTok Shop](https://www.gelato.com/print-on-demand/tiktok-shop); [Gelato blog](https://www.gelato.com/blog/how-to-sell-on-tiktok-shop-with-print-on-demand); [printkk TikTok guide](https://www.printkk.com/blog/articles/print-on-demand-with-tiktok-shop); [Gelato integrations](https://www.gelato.com/integrations)

**Worked example (inference using the cited figures above)**
- A $25 tee on Etsy with a Printify base cost of $9.04, before shipping and ads: Etsy fees are about $0.20 + $1.63 (6.5%) + about $1.00 (3% + $0.25), or about $2.83. That leaves about $13.1, roughly 52% gross before shipping and ads. If 15% Offsite Ads apply (-$3.75), the margin falls to about 37%.

### Inferences
- Printify's low base costs on mugs and home goods make them attractive for Etsy. Printful and Gelato cost more but reduce the risk of quality complaints and poor reviews.

### Gaps
- No comparable Printful or Gelato base prices for rugs, doormats, or blankets were found. Printify prices come from catalog snippets, some in EUR, and may change.
- No 2026 data on POD return or defect rates by provider.

## 4. AI image tools used by POD sellers in 2026 and their licensing

### Takeaway
The common 2026 toolkit is Midjourney for artistic quality, Ideogram for text and typography, GPT Image and Nano Banana (Gemini) for general use, Flux (via paid APIs), Recraft for vectors and transparent PNGs, Adobe Firefly for the safest legal position, and Kittl or Canva for layout. Licensing is usually fine on paid tiers but varies. Midjourney's free trial is non-commercial, Recraft's free plan does not allow commercial use, and Flux [dev] weights are non-commercial even though their outputs can be used commercially.

### Cited Findings
- Clear commercial rights are granted by ChatGPT, Midjourney (Basic and up), Nano Banana (paid), Ideogram (paid), and Grok Imagine. Recraft Pro ($10/mo) grants full commercial rights, but Recraft owns the rights to free-plan images. — [nesyona/PickAI POD comparison](https://nesyona.com/articles/best-ai-image-generators-print-on-demand-2026); [Rangy 2026 guide](https://rangy.ai/blog/ai-image-generators-2026-complete-guide/)
- For POD: Firefly is "the most legally defensible" because it is the only one offering formal IP indemnification. Recraft is "the strongest end-to-end POD pipeline" thanks to native SVG and transparent PNG export. Midjourney has the highest quality but carries unresolved litigation risk. Ideogram 3 leads on text in images. Recraft V3 is the only native vector model. Nano Banana Pro leads on photorealism. — [nesyona/PickAI](https://nesyona.com/articles/best-ai-image-generators-print-on-demand-2026); [FrankX 2026](https://www.frankx.ai/blog/best-ai-image-generators-2026)
- Midjourney: paid subscribers own their outputs with full commercial rights. Companies with more than $1M gross annual revenue must use the Pro or Mega plan. Free-trial images are licensed only under CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial). Commentary notes risk after the Disney lawsuit. — [terms.law Midjourney](https://terms.law/ai-output-rights/midjourney/); [terms.law 2026 guide](https://terms.law/2026/01/15/midjourney-commercial-use-rights-complete-2026-guide/)
- Ideogram claims no ownership of outputs, and commercial use is allowed subject to third-party rights and the AUP. Secondary sources say this applies to all tiers, free included. Note that the free tier makes generations public. — [Ideogram licensing](https://ideogram.ai/licensing/); [Ideogram ToS](https://ideogram.ai/legal/tos/); [Ideogram FAQ](https://docs.ideogram.ai/frequently-asked-questions); [whathumansask](https://whathumansask.com/q/does-ideogram-allow-commercial-use-on-free-plan/)
- The FLUX.1 [dev] license says: "We claim no ownership rights in and to the Outputs … You may use Output for any purpose (including for commercial purposes)." The model itself may only be used for non-commercial purposes unless you have a paid license. A Hugging Face discussion says the Kontext-dev v1.1 license removed the "commercial outputs" line, so check the current terms. — [FLUX.1-dev LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-dev/blob/main/LICENSE.md); [BFL licensing](https://bfl.ai/licensing); [HF Kontext discussion](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-Kontext-dev/discussions/6)
- A common Claude workflow: Claude generates the design ideas and image prompts, Kittl creates the images, and prompts include "flat graphic design, no background, transparent PNG, print on demand ready." — [YouTube: 100 trending designs with Claude](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=956rB728VJk); [Magica summary](https://magica.com/youtube-summarizer/how-to-create-100-trending-t-shirt-designs-in-10-minutes-using-claude-ai-and-etsy-research-956rB728VJk)

### Inferences
- Paid tiers are the safe default. The free tiers of Midjourney and Recraft are not usable for commercial POD.
- Transparent backgrounds and resolution matter a lot for POD printing (a tee needs about 4500x5400 px). Recraft's vector output avoids the need for upscaling.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve 2026 licensing pages for GPT Image, Leonardo, Kittl, or Canva, or for upscalers and background removers (Topaz, Upscayl, remove.bg, Photoroom). Their terms should be checked directly.
- No primary data on which tool POD sellers use most (no survey found).

## 6. Copyright and trademark risk

### Takeaway
Purely AI-generated designs cannot be copyrighted in the US. The Supreme Court declined Thaler v. Perlmutter on March 2, 2026, and the Copyright Office says prompts alone are not enough. POD sellers therefore usually cannot stop others from copying pure-AI designs, while they remain fully liable if their designs infringe others' trademarks or copyrights.

### Cited Findings
- The US Copyright Office's January 2025 report (Part 2) says "prompts essentially function as instructions that convey unprotectable ideas." Current tools do not give enough control for prompts alone to support copyright. Human selection, arrangement, or modification can be protected. — [Lexology 2026 update](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=1c80f0d3-c6b5-4b2d-af10-77baa816751d); [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/ce8eaa5f/ai-in-litigation-series-an-update-on-ai-copyright-cases-in-2026)
- On March 2, 2026 the Supreme Court denied cert in Thaler v. Perlmutter, leaving in place the D.C. Circuit's human-authorship requirement (decided March 2025). How much human input is enough was not addressed. — [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/supreme-court-denies-review-in-ai-authorship-case); [Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/viewpoints/102mlpl/supreme-court-denies-certiorari-in-thaler-v-perlmutter-human-only-rule-for-ai/); [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/the-final-word-supreme-court-refuses-to-hear-case-on-ai-authorship); [D.C. Cir. opinion](https://media.cadc.uscourts.gov/opinions/docs/2025/03/23-5233.pdf)
- Trademark screening: run a USPTO clearance check before printing any brand name or catchphrase. Tools include Merch Informer, Niche Hunter Pro (USPTO checker extension), Tangent TESS Helper, and Trademark Bulk (CSV bulk checker focused on Class 025 apparel). — [Printify: copyright vs trademark](https://printify.com/knowledge-hub/copyright-vs-trademark-print-on-demand/); [Niche Hunter Pro](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bfabjhghkpjofoajcpnnbmhjajoloebm); [Tangent TESS](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/tangent-tess/bpffpimmpcohabnlfnmnidhengehjpmc); [Trademark Bulk](https://www.producthunt.com/products/trademark-bulk); [Merch Informer USPTO tutorial](https://merchinformer.com/?p=7060)
- The usual escalation for infringement on POD platforms is DMCA takedown, then listing removal, then account freeze with payouts frozen, then the catalog wiped. — [search snippet, Printify knowledge hub context](https://printify.com/knowledge-hub/copyright-vs-trademark-print-on-demand/)

### Inferences
- Human editing in Illustrator or Kittl, or Claude-written SVG shaped by specific human choices, strengthens a seller's copyright position somewhat. Registration is still uncertain.
- LLM-generated slogans can accidentally reproduce trademarked phrases. Every Claude-suggested slogan should go through a USPTO/TESS check (TMHunt, Trademark Bulk, or similar).

### Gaps
- I did not find a current primary page for "TMHunt" specifically. It is a known POD trademark-check tool, but no 2026 source was retrieved.
- No data on how often POD sellers receive IP strikes.

## 7. Realistic earnings, saturation, and success rates

### Takeaway
Most sources describe POD as saturated. Typical reported earnings are small: $50-300/month for beginners and $1-3k/month for consistent sellers after a year or more. Hyper-specific niches are the main route to success, and survivorship bias is strong. No rigorous seller survey on success or failure rates was found.

- Beginners typically earn $50-300/month. Consistent sellers reach $1,000-2,000+/month, and some reach $1-3k/month with sustained effort. — [Artomate](https://www.artomate.app/blog/etsy-print-on-demand-side-hustle-2026-earnings-how-to-start); [Bents Magazine](https://bentsmagazine.co.uk/etsy-print-on-demand-in-2026-can-you-really-make-money-or-is-it-overhyped/)

### Inferences
- AI cuts the cost of producing designs close to zero, so design volume is no longer an advantage. Niche insight, brand, traffic (TikTok or Pinterest), and compliance are.
- Etsy's flat buyer count combined with 5.9% seller growth (section 1) supports the view that it is saturated.
