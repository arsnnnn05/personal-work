# NextAutomation — what the company does (from repos)

Sources: arsnnnn05/nextautomation-pipeline-scraping-bot (README, docs/GUIDE_COLLABORATEURS.md, pipeline/STRATEGY.md, docs/*.pdf/*.docx, pipeline/voice/*), LucasNextAutomation/na-video-studio.

- Positioning: "done-for-you AI implementation partner for real estate". Tagline "Hire Intelligence, Not Headcount". Sells against labor budgets ($60K/yr assistant), not software budgets ($300/mo CRM).
- Two revenue motions:
  1. Agency / custom builds: lead gen, follow-up, CRM, voice AI, reporting for RE developers, investors, agents/brokers, B2B SMBs. 50+ proposal pages, 3 case studies (Alure Capital, DNX Consulting, Strales). Salary anchoring: "replaces a $7–15K/mo analyst".
  2. Product: "AI Employees" platform (launch July 2026) — 15 named AI employees: Scout (voice AI calls inbound leads in <60s, qualifies, books), Nora (transaction coordinator / follow-up), Vera (market analyst / content). Pricing $99 / $299 / $799 / Enterprise. Stack: Retell AI + Telnyx (voice), Claude API, Modal. 40+ integrations.
  3. Community: Skool "AI Sphere" paid €50–150/mo, courses + prompt libraries per RE vertical; Zapier→Brevo nurture sequence.
- Marketing machine: daily scraping pipeline (RSS/HN/Reddit) → local LLM scoring (Gemma) → strategy eval → judge → DeepSeek generation of 5 platform posts → Supabase approval queue; weekly newsletter (AutomateRE), LinkedIn founder-led content, video studio (HyperFrames/HeyGen/Remotion) for short explainer videos ("Computer Use for brokerages").
- Competitive method: 49-entity deep dive, finding the gap (no incumbent does autonomous voice AI; no one frames AI as employees).
- Core transferable assets: (a) vertical-specific pain mapping, (b) speed-to-lead voice AI, (c) scrapers for lead lists, (d) CRM + follow-up automations, (e) content pipeline + founder-led audience, (f) community/education funnel, (g) productization into named AI employees.
