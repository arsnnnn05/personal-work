# Etsy + Printify niche shortlist: AI-assisted illustration shop, France-based, EU-first

*Researched 2026-09-30. The shop sells AI-assisted illustrations that a person then edits, to buyers mostly in France and Germany plus other countries.*

---

## 0. How to read this (method and data caveats)

- **Blocked sources:** The network proxy blocked direct fetches of Etsy, eRank, RankHero and InsightAgent. All numbers below come from **search-result snippets** of those pages, not from pages I read in full. Treat them as rough figures.
- **What RankHero's numbers mean:** RankHero keyword pages report "monthly searches" that look like **Google search volumes**, not Etsy's own searches. For example, it gives "robin bird" 110,000 and "french bakery" 135,000, which is clearly web-wide intent. Its **"competing listings"** figure is the useful number because it counts Etsy listings.
  - Use volume to compare niches and to see which way demand is moving.
  - Use listing counts to judge how saturated a niche is.
- **French-language data is thin:** I found no French-language Etsy keyword volumes newer than 2024 (eRank's French monthly reports stop then). Where I say French or German keywords are "less crowded", the one hard data point is **"dackel"**: 82% of its demand is in the EU, only 1,349 Etsy listings compete for it, and it rates 8/100 difficulty. That contrast with the English terms supports the bilingual thesis. For the other niches it is an informed inference. Check it yourself before committing:
  1. Type each keyword into Etsy.fr or Etsy.de search.
  2. Note the "X résultats" count.
  3. Compare it with the English equivalent.

### Platform context that applies to all five niches

- **AI disclosure is mandatory.** Etsy's updated seller policy took effect 14 Jan 2026. For each listing you must:
  - tick the "I used AI-generative technology" box;
  - choose "Designed by a seller";
  - state the AI use in the description;
  - disclose the production partner (Printify).

  Etsy also removed the "templated design" allowance on 10 Jun 2025, so the design must come from you. ([beancount.io summary](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/09/etsy-2026-fee-hike-ai-disclosure-rules-handmade-sellers-guide), [ecombalance](https://ecombalance.com/ai-content-policies-2026/), [Printify blog on Etsy IP](https://printify.com/blog/etsy-copyright-infringement/))
- **Generic AI art is saturated and buyers distrust it.** There are reports of "AI slop" fatigue. Commentators give the same advice: specialise and disclose. Their example: "'AI art' is a saturated commodity, 'vintage botanical AI prints for nursery walls' is a business." ([Unilink, 2026](https://www.unilink.us/blog/how-to-sell-ai-art-2026), [Jeweller Magazine](https://www.jewellermagazine.com/Article/15316/Etsy-facing-long-term-headache-because-of-AI))
  - Your human editing should be visible: consistent line weight, a fixed palette, hand-lettered French or German labels, and a recognisable house style.
  - Do not sell raw Midjourney output.
- **Use EU fulfilment.** Since 1 Jul 2026 the EU charges €3 per commodity type on parcels under €150 shipped from outside the EU, and about €2 more per parcel is expected from Nov 2026. Orders fulfilled inside the EU are exempt.
  - In Printify's catalogue, filter by "Ships from: Europe" and only publish products that have an EU print provider.
  - EU delivery takes about 4–10 business days.
  - Sources: [Printify EU customs](https://printify.com/blog/eu-customs-duty/), [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/ie/en/insights/news-opinions/eu-3-duty-on-low-value-imports), [Printify Europe](https://printify.com/print-on-demand/europe)
- **Translate listings manually.** Etsy's own guidance says buyers trust manual translations more and that manually translated listings outsell machine-translated ones. Write your FR and DE titles and tags yourself; don't rely on auto-translate. ([Etsy Help: translate listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000343048), [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/679881202901))
- **The US is still the majority of Etsy sales**, about 75% in Q1 2025 ([Branvas stats](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/etsy-statistics-sellers-buyers-gms-fees), [LinkMyBooks](https://linkmybooks.com/blog/how-many-sellers-are-on-etsy)). A French-only shop caps your audience. The best niches below work **bilingually**: a French or German design in front, with English tags as well.
- **Trend backdrop for Q4 2026:**
  - Etsy's 2026 Colour of the Year is **Patina Blue** and its Texture of the Year is **Washed Linen**. Etsy frames both around items that feel "lived-in, passed down" ([Living Etc](https://www.livingetc.com/news/etsy-announces-color-and-texture-of-year-2026), [Stylist](https://www.stylist.co.uk/home/etsy-colour-of-the-year-2026/1044077)).
  - Etsy's fall and holiday trend reports name **Gothmas, Nonna Holiday, Nutcrackercore and Supper Club**. Table-setting searches were reported up about 1,000% and glassware up 12x ([Etsy Seller Handbook holiday report](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1417223353768), [eRank fall 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/fall-2026-trends-etsy-sellers/), [SellerApp](https://www.sellerapp.com/blog/etsy-trends/)).
  - Pinterest Predicts 2026 includes **Cool Blue**, **Neo Deco**, and a move away from minimalism towards "nostalgic, playful" interiors ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/pinterest-publishes-2026-trend-predictions-report/807452/), [NBC Select](https://www.nbcnews.com/select/shopping/pinterest-predicts-2026-trend-report-rcna248706)).

## 2. Niche profiles

### #1 Illustrated Teckel / Dackel (dachshund) gifts, bilingual DE/FR, with a vintage-illustration style

**Why it ranks first:** It has the clearest measured evidence that a non-English keyword space is under-served. It also sells well as a gift, is illustration-led, and peaks in Q4.

**Demand**
- **"dackel"** (the German word): about 165,000 monthly searches (web-wide), **82% from the EU**. Only **1,349 Etsy listings** compete, difficulty 8/100, median price about $24 ([RankHero: dackel](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/dackel)).
- **"dachshund christmas"**: 1,900 average monthly searches, **peaking at about 12,100 in Dec 2025** and building through the autumn. There are 31,338 competing listings, difficulty 42 ([RankHero: dachshund christmas](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/dachshund-christmas)).
- **"dachshund mug"**: 4,400 monthly searches, 7,187 listings, difficulty 31 ([RankHero: dachshund mug](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/dachshund-mug)).
- The dachshund re-entered **Germany's top-10 dog breeds in 2025** ([t-online](https://www.t-online.de/leben/aktuelles/id_101097240/hunde-ranking-2025-das-sind-die-beliebtesten-rassen-der-deutschen.html), [web.de](https://web.de/magazine/wissen/tiere/beliebtesten-hunderassen-deutschlands-2025-41437864), [zooplus](https://www.zooplus.de/magazin/hund/hundehaltung/beliebte-hunderassen)).
- It is evergreen, with seasonal spikes at Christmas, Valentine's and Mother's Day ("Dackel Mama").

**Competition**
- English-language dachshund merch is crowded, with many text-only "wiener dog" shirts.
- German-language long-tails are nearly empty:
  - "dackel wandbild": 30 searches, **0 listings**;
  - "dackel mama": 20 searches, 145 listings ([RankHero: dackel wandbild](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/dackel-wandbild), [RankHero: dackel mama](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/dackel-mama)).
- Search volumes on those long-tails are tiny, so group many of them into one listing's 13 tags.
- **Unverified:** French "teckel" volume and listing count. I found no data; check Etsy.fr.

**Illustration fit:** Excellent.
- The dog's shape makes it an instant visual gag: a long dog curled round a mug, a dachshund as a draught-excluder, in a scarf.
- A consistent vintage or gouache character style would stand apart from photo-style AI dogs.

**EU and bilingual angle**
- German is primary, where Dackel is part of national culture (the Munich "Dackelmuseum", Bavarian cliché).
- French is secondary ("teckel", "saucisse").
- Put English tags in as well.

**Best Printify products** (choose EU providers)
1. **Mugs** (11oz or 15oz, possibly enamel/camping mugs). They are the classic €15–25 gift and "dachshund mug" demand is proven.
2. **Sweatshirts and hoodies** for "Dackel Mama" and winter Christmas designs. They carry a higher price and suit Q4.
3. **Tote bags** and **posters** (the "dackel wandbild" gap). A **pet bandana** can be an add-on if an EU provider offers one.

**Trademark and IP traps**
- **Waldi**, the striped dachshund that was mascot of the 1972 Munich Olympics. Olympic symbols and names are protected in Germany under a dedicated law, and "Olympia" is off-limits ([DPMA on Olympic IP](https://dpma.de/dpma/veroeffentlichungen/hintergrund/sportfreizeitundip/olympia/index.html), [munich.travel on Waldi](https://www.munich.travel/artikel/sport-freizeit/tipps-auf-den-spuren-der-olympischen-sommerspiele-1972)).
  - Do not make a striped rainbow dachshund in the Waldi style.
- **Brand parody:**
  - Oscar Mayer / Wienermobile hot-dog jokes.
  - "Haribo"-style gummy dachshunds.
  - Lidl/Aldi parodies.
  - Beer brands for "Dackel & Bier" designs.
  - Disney's dachshund (*Lady and the Tramp*), "Slinky Dog" (Toy Story), and *Sausage Party*.
- **"Dachshund Through the Snow"** is also the title of a children's book. I could not confirm whether it is a registered trademark ([book record](https://catalog.ocln.org/Record/418736)). Before using any pun phrase, check USPTO (tmsearch.uspto.gov), EUIPO (euipo.europa.eu/eSearch) and DPMA.
- **Personal names:** Custom pet names are fine. Celebrity dogs are not, for example Crusoe the Celebrity Dachshund.

**5 design concepts**
1. **"Frohe Weihnachten vom Dackel"**: a long-bodied dachshund wrapped round a Christmas tree like a garland, in a vintage gouache style. Use it on a mug and a sweatshirt.
2. **"Teckel Club de Paris, depuis 1888"**: a 1950s French travel-poster pastiche with a dachshund in a beret on a Vespa, using a *fictional* club name. Use it on a poster and a tote.
3. **"Dackel-Anatomie"**: a vintage scientific plate with a tongue-in-cheek labelled diagram ("Snack-Radar", "Bauchkontakt mit Boden"). Make a German and a French version. Use it on a poster and a mug.
4. **Coat-type trio**: short-haired, long-haired and wire-haired dachshunds in Patina Blue (Etsy's 2026 Colour of the Year). The buyer chooses the coat and colour from a variation menu. Use it on mugs and sweatshirts.
5. **"Dackel Mama / Maman de teckel"**: a line-art dachshund with a botanical wreath and room for a custom name, via Printify personalisation. Use it on a tote and a sweatshirt.

### #2 Vintage-naturalist mushroom plates for foragers ("planche naturaliste champignons" / "Pilzkunde")

**Why it ranks second:** It is timely right now, since French mushroom season peaks in October. It is also evergreen, fits illustration perfectly, and the French and German forager culture is real. It loses to #1 because English "mushroom" art is **extremely saturated**, with many free public-domain plates and a lot of whimsical AI mushroom art.

**Demand**
- "mushroom decor": 5,400 monthly searches, **146,026 listings**, 67% US ([RankHero: mushroom decor](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/mushroom-decor)).
- "mushroom wall art": 480 searches, **64,606 listings**, difficulty 66 (hard) ([RankHero: mushroom wall art](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/mushroom-wall-art)).
- "mushroom ornament": searches **+60%** year on year from Feb 2025 to Jan 2026 ([RankHero: mushroom ornament](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/mushroom-ornament)).
- "whimsical mushroom": +22% ([RankHero: whimsical mushroom](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/whimsical-mushroom)).
- **Evidence of foraging in France:**
  - ANSES recorded about 500 poisonings from 1 Jul 2025, rising from September and expected to peak in October ([ANSES](https://www.anses.fr/fr/content/cueillette-des-champignons-vigilance-face-aux-risques-dintoxications)).
  - A bumper 2025 season got national press coverage ([JDN](https://www.journaldunet.com/magazine/1546081-rv1-pousse-exceptionnelle-champignon/)).
- The niche is evergreen with an autumn peak, and fits the "forest Christmas" / Gothmas look well.

**Competition**
- Heavy in English.
- Vintage mushroom charts in the public domain are everywhere, and 1stDibs sells originals ([1stDibs vintage mushroom print](https://www.1stdibs.com/buy/vintage-mushroom-print)). Buyers can get restored scans cheaply, so **a restored-scan look will not win**.
- To win, focus on **specific French or German regional species sets with authentic vernacular and Latin labels** that you letter yourself:
  - "Les cèpes du Périgord"
  - "Champignons des Vosges"
  - "Pilze des Schwarzwalds"
- Also a forager's humour angle.

**Illustration fit:** Excellent. Botanical plate, woodcut, gouache and Art Nouveau styles all suit it. Human editing matters: species must be **accurate**, because foragers spot mistakes.

**EU and bilingual angle:** Strong. Foraging is a mainstream family pastime in France and Germany, unlike the "aesthetic mushroom" market in the US.

**Best Printify products**
1. **Posters and art prints**: species plates and regional sets.
2. **Tote bags and canvas bags**, framed as "sac de cueillette". This is a gift that forages can actually use. Note: a tote is not a basket, so be honest in the copy.
3. **Mugs** and **tea towels / kitchen towels** if an EU provider exists, for kitchen décor. Ceramic **ornaments** could be a Q4 test if an EU provider offers them.

**Trademark and IP traps**
- **Drugs:** Etsy bans drugs and drug paraphernalia. Avoid "magic mushroom", psilocybin and psychedelic framing, and the fly agaric as a "trip" motif ([Etsy prohibited items](https://www.etsy.com/legal/prohibited), [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360024112614)).
- **Characters:**
  - Super Mario's mushroom (Nintendo).
  - The Smurfs' mushroom houses (Peyo / IMPS, which enforces actively in Francophone markets).
  - Alice in Wonderland's caterpillar on a mushroom in the Disney style. Tenniel's originals are public domain; the Disney designs are not.
  - Animal Crossing and Ghibli styles.
- **Safety and liability:** Never present a design as an identification guide. Add a note such as "décoratif, ne pas utiliser pour l'identification". A wrong "comestible" label is a real risk.
- **Copyright:** Do not trace modern field-guide plates. Prompts "in the style of" a living illustrator also carry risk.

**5 design concepts**
1. **"Les Champignons de nos forêts"**: a 12-species plate (cèpe, girolle, morille, trompette de la mort, pied-de-mouton…) with Latin and French names in hand-drawn lettering and aged paper. Use it on a poster.
2. **"Le panier du cueilleur"**: an overhead illustration of a wicker basket full of cèpes and girolles, with the forager motto "Qui cherche trouve". Use it on a tote and a mug.
3. **"Pilze des Waldes"**: the German counterpart (Steinpilz, Pfifferling, Maronenröhrling), in Art Nouveau Jugendstil. Use it on a poster and a tote.
4. **Gothmas forest Christmas**: dark-green and black mushroom wreath with candlelight and snow. This ties to Etsy's Gothmas trend. Use it on a poster and a mug.
5. **"Calendrier de cueillette"**: a circular month-by-month wheel showing which mushroom is in season, as decoration only. Use it on a poster and a tea towel.

**10 long-tail keywords (FR / DE / EN)**
1. affiche champignons vintage
2. planche naturaliste champignons
3. cadeau cueilleur champignons
4. tote bag champignons cèpes
5. mug champignon forêt
6. poster pilze vintage
7. pilze bestimmung poster deko
8. geschenk pilzsammler
9. vintage mushroom botanical print french
10. mushroom forager gift tote bag

### #3 European garden and winter birds: robin, tits, bullfinch, blackbird ("rouge-gorge", "mésange", "Rotkehlchen", "Gimpel")

**Why it ranks third:** The demand story is excellent: birding is booming among young people in the UK and Germany, and the robin is a Christmas icon in Europe. Competition for generic "bird art" is very heavy, though, so success depends on a tight sub-niche.

**Demand**
- UK: Gen Z birdwatchers **+1,088% since 2018**; all-age participation +47% ([YPulse 2026](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2026/05/06/british-gen-z-is-driving-a-birdwatching-boom/), [Rare Bird Alert](https://www.rarebirdalert.co.uk/v2/Content/Gen_Z_birdwatching_numbers_rise_tenfold_since_2018.aspx)).
- Germany: a record 6,000 new ornitho.de users in 2025 ([Utopia.de](https://utopia.de/news/birding-gen-z-entdeckt-neuen-trend-der-dem-artenschutz-hilft_931006/)).
- "bird art": 8,100 searches. "bird illustration": 4,400. "robin gifts": 3,600, difficulty 41 ([RankHero: bird art](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/bird-art), [RankHero: robin gifts](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/robin-gifts)).
- The robin is a Christmas symbol from the Victorian era onward. Robin Christmas cards are a big seller at charities such as the RSPB ([RSPB shop](https://shopping.rspb.org.uk/christmas-shop/charity-christmas-cards/robin-christmas-cards)). However, one industry count found robins on Christmas cards down 29% over a decade ([PG Buzz](https://pgbuzz.net/29-less-robins-27-more-glitter-and-slimmed-down-santas-reflect-changing-face-of-christmas-cards)). **Mixed signal:** the robin is iconic but not a growing trend on its own.

**Competition**
- "bird art print": **349,491 listings**, difficulty 74 (hard) ([RankHero: bird art print](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/bird-art-print)).
- Generic bird art is off-limits for a new shop.
- The opening is **European species with French or German names** plus winter-feeding, garden or birdwatcher-humour angles. Much Etsy bird art shows *American* species (northern cardinal, American robin), which is an obvious gap for EU buyers.

**Illustration fit:** Excellent. It suits watercolour, linocut, and vintage "planche ornithologique" styles.

**EU and bilingual angle:** Good. The robin is iconic in the UK, France and Germany. Christmas cards themselves are not a Printify strength, so use décor and gift products instead.

**Best Printify products**
1. **Mugs**: the "tasse du matin" birdwatcher gift.
2. **Posters**: "Oiseaux du jardin" species charts, which are evergreen.
3. **Sweatshirts** (small left-chest robin embroidery-style print) or **tote bags**. Consider **doormats** with a "Bienvenue aux mésanges" style design if an EU provider offers them.

**Trademark and IP traps**
- Charity and NGO names and logos:
  - **RSPB**, **LPO** (Ligue pour la Protection des Oiseaux), **NABU**, and NABU's campaigns "Stunde der Wintervögel" / "Stunde der Gartenvögel";
  - Audubon (National Audubon Society);
  - the Merlin / eBird apps.
- Angry Birds (Rovio) and the old Twitter bird logo.
- **Copyrighted field-guide art:** Do not copy or prompt from modern plates such as the Collins Bird Guide illustrators. Audubon's and Gould's 19th-century plates are public domain, but everyone uses them.
- Batman's "Robin" in word-play designs.

**5 design concepts**
1. **"Les oiseaux de mon jardin"**: a 12-species chart of the rouge-gorge, mésange bleue, mésange charbonnière, merle, moineau, pinson and others, with French names and a vintage naturalist plate look. Make a German version, "Gartenvögel". Use it on a poster and a tea towel.
2. **"Noël au jardin"**: a robin on a snowy holly branch with the bird feeder visible, in a gouache Christmas style. Use it on a mug and a sweatshirt.
3. **"Club des observateurs d'oiseaux, est. 2026"**: a retro badge of binoculars and a bullfinch. This is the birder-identity angle for a young audience. Use it on a sweatshirt and a tote.
4. **"Mangeoire d'hiver"**: tits and a bullfinch arguing over a fat ball, as a humorous vignette. Use it on a mug.
5. **Winter bird calendar wreath**: 12 birds in a circle, one per month. Use it on a poster.

### #4 Alpine après-ski and vintage ski-village illustrations (French, Swiss and Austrian Alps; Pyrenees)

**Why it ranks fourth:** The seasonal timing for Q4 and Q1 is the best of the five, the trend is current, and the European audience is ready-made. It ranks below #1–3 because it is **strongly seasonal** (dead from May to August) and has a **specific trademark trap** in resort names.

**Demand**
- "vintage ski poster": 3,600 monthly searches, **16,073 listings**, difficulty 36 (achievable). It **peaked in November 2025**, and 53% of demand is from the US ([RankHero: vintage ski poster](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/vintage-ski-poster)).
- "apres ski hoodie": 1,000 searches against **only 2,157 listings**, difficulty 32 ([RankHero: apres ski hoodie](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/apres-ski-hoodie)).
- Other ski searches, with their competing listings:

  | Keyword | Monthly searches | Listings |
  |---|---|---|
  | "ski hat" | 6,600 | 24,277 |
  | "skiing shirt" | 3,600 | 25,081 |

- Home media report the **"après-ski" and ski-Christmas décor micro-trend**, with Pinterest searches up about 29% ([Ideal Home](https://www.idealhome.co.uk/all-rooms/apres-ski-microtrend), [Homes & Gardens](https://www.homesandgardens.com/interior-design/ski-christmas-decor-trend), [Apartment Therapy](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/apres-ski-core-decor-trend-37435734)).

**Competition:** Moderate. Much of it is restored public-domain posters, and English "après ski" slogan shirts are common. Illustrated **generic Alpine scenes with French or German lettering** ("Les sports d'hiver", "Winterfrische") look less crowded, but this is unverified.

**Illustration fit:** Excellent. 1930s–60s poster style suits it: flat colour, bold skier silhouettes, and chalets.

**EU and bilingual angle:** Good. French, German, Austrian and Swiss buyers have a personal link to "their" Alps, and chalet owners and rentals buy décor.

**Best Printify products**
1. **Posters** (framed where an EU provider offers framing).
2. **Hoodies and sweatshirts**, following the "après ski hoodie" gap.
3. **Mugs**, plus **blankets / throws** for the chalet ("Cocooning au chalet") if the EU margin allows.

**Trademark and IP traps (important)**
- **Resort names are often registered trademarks**, and resort operators litigate. Aspen Skiing Co. sued the skiwear brand Perfect Moment ([GearJunkie](https://gearjunkie.com/winter/aspen-skiing-company-sues-perfect-moment), [KPCW](https://www.kpcw.org/ski-resorts/2024-01-01/aspen-resort-suing-brand-for-ski-beach-clothing-influencer-photos)). I could not verify the French resort registrations (Courchevel, Val d'Isère, Val Thorens, Megève and others) in the INPI database for this report.
  - Before using any resort name, search INPI (data.inpi.fr) and EUIPO.
  - Safer alternatives: **mountain and geographic names** (Mont Blanc, Pyrénées, Tyrol), **invented villages**, and generic "Station de ski".
- **Brands:** Moncler, Rossignol, Salomon, Fusalp, Ellesse, and Toblerone's Matterhorn packaging.
- **Tintin** (Moulinsart is very aggressive) and similar characters.
- **Vintage-poster artists' copyright:** Copyright lasts 70 years after the artist's death. Many 1950s–60s poster artists are still under copyright, and SNCF/PLM railway logos are trademarks.
  - Create original compositions; do not re-draw a specific famous poster.

**5 design concepts**
1. **"Les Sports d'Hiver"**: a 1930s-style skier mid-turn above an invented village, "Saint-Clair-les-Neiges". Use it on a poster.
2. **"Après-ski Club, depuis 1968"**: a retro badge with crossed skis, a fondue pot and a vin chaud mug. Use it on a hoodie and a mug.
3. **"Fondue & Raclette Society"**: a cheese-pull illustration in chalet colours. This bridges to niche #5. Use it on a mug and a tote.
4. **"Pyrénées, l'hiver"**: a regional scene of an izard (Pyrenean chamois) on a snowy ridge, which suits Pyrenees locals and is less crowded than the Alps. Use it on a poster.

**Why it ranks fifth:** It is on-trend with the Supper Club and Nonna Holiday trends. **Best Printify products**

2. **Posters**: kitchen wall art.
3. **Mugs** and **tote bags** ("sac de marché").

**Trademark and IP traps (important)**
- **Protected designations of origin (PDO/AOC).** "Champagne" is enforced aggressively, even against *non-wine* goods and services. The CJEU ruled in C-783/19, the *Champanillo* case, that "evocation" of a PDO does not require similar products ([Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/en/spain/art/protecting-designations-of-origin-and-the-concept-of-evocation-the-champanillo-case), [Cerha Hempel](https://www.cerhahempel.com/blog/food-law/the-name-champanillo-for-a-tapas-bar-is-an-undue-evocation-of-the-protected-designation-of-origin-pdo-champagne)). My assessment:
  - **Avoid "Champagne"** entirely.
  - Take care with other PDO names such as Roquefort and Comté on commercial designs. Descriptive use in an illustrated cheese chart is probably lower risk, but it is not risk-free.
- **Brand posters** you might be tempted to pastiche:
  - La Vache qui rit (Bel);
  - Ricard, Pastis 51, Lillet, Orangina, Badoit;
  - Michelin's Bibendum;
  - Banania, which also carries racist imagery;

  - Nutella;
  - Ladurée's macaron packaging.
- **Alcohol:** Etsy bans alcohol products. Wine-themed *art* is fine, but do not imply that you sell alcohol.
- **Printify listing rules:** Keep titles free of brand names, including "Le Creuset style".

## 3. Why this order

1. **Teckel/Dackel** comes first because it is the **only niche with hard data** showing that the EU-language long-tail is both in demand and under-supplied (82% EU demand, about 1.3k listings). It also meets all the other criteria: gift-driven, Q4 peak, illustration-led, bilingual. Its main risk is drifting into the crowded English "wiener dog" merch; stay DE/FR-first.
2. **Mushrooms** comes second because the season matches today exactly and the forager culture in FR/DE is real. The English market is badly saturated, so it only works with specific, accurate regional French or German content, which rewards your human editing.
3. **European birds** comes third because the demand trend (the birding boom) is the strongest *long-term* signal of the five. Generic bird art is very crowded (349k listings), and the robin Christmas motif is iconic but flat to declining, so it needs careful positioning (European species, French and German names, birder identity).
4. **Après-ski** comes fourth because its timing and competition numbers are attractive (16k listings; hoodies about 2k). It is only fully useful for about five months a year, and the resort-name trademark trap needs research before each design.
5. **French table & apéro** comes fifth because it is on-trend, and a French seller has an authenticity edge. The market is crowded with pseudo-French, the product fit depends on EU availability of tea towels and aprons, the PDO/brand legal traps are the most technical of the five, and my competition data is the thinnest here.

**Suggested launch sequence** (end of September start)
1. Launch **#1 and #2 now**: mushrooms for October, and dachshund Christmas designs listed by mid-October so they are indexed before the December peak.
2. Add **#4** in early November for the ski season.
3. Add **#3 robins and winter birds** in November, then keep birds as the evergreen core for 2027.
4. Test **#5** as a small Christmas hosting collection.

Keep a single shop identity, for example "vintage European naturalist and heritage illustration". Dachshunds, mushrooms, birds and Alpine scenes can all sit under one house style.
