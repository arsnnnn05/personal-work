## Production partner (Shop Manager › Settings › Production partners › Add)

- Location: **Czech Republic**
- About: **"They print and ship my designs on mugs and other products."** Choose *"They help me make my designs"* / printing.

- Returns & exchanges: **accept returns, 14 days**. Buyer pays return shipping unless the item is defective.
- Damaged or misprinted items: replaced for free; send a photo within 30 days.
- Processing time: **3–5 business days** · Shipping: **free**, EU 5–10 business days.
